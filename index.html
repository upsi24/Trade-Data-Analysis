How the model learns and counts pips

The model matches the 15 most similar prior candles using normalized body size, 3-bar drift, closing location, and range expansion. When at least 10 of 15 moved in the same direction by the next close, it projects a paper BUY or SELL; otherwise it signals WAIT.

Scalping Signal & Indicator Guide

How the Real-Time M1 / M5 / M15 Signal Engine Works

The monitor streams live XAUUSD ticks and closed M1 candles from Deriv WebSocket and aggregates aligned M5 and M15 candles (supplemented with direct M15 history when needed). Every tick and candle close updates a weighted probability model: M1 scalping momentum (30%), M5 structure (30%), M15 trend filter (20%), k-NN historical pattern similarity (15%), and live spot pressure (5%).

When it shows WAIT: If M1 and M5 disagree, EMA 9/21 separation is too flat (choppy market), RSI is exhausted (>76 for Buy or <24 for Sell), probability is below 62%, or the data feed is delayed/closed, the system blocks entry and displays WAIT alongside the next probable lean and breakout trigger price.

Stop Loss & Collect Profit: Stops combine recent 5-bar swing highs/lows with ATR(14) volatility buffers. Collect Profit (TP1) targets 1.5× risk and Extended Profit (TP2) targets 2.5× risk. All signals are analytical decision aids—always verify broker spreads, news events, and risk limits before trading live capital.
