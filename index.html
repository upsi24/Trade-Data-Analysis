Trade journal

Historical entry review

Practice on past Gold/USD candles with a $10,000 starting balance. Choose M1 or M5 to study a past entry. Note the candle’s range, record a paper position, then advance the replay to see how the price changed. The replay also advances at the selected interval while this view is open. You can also move forward manually. Fills use the displayed close and exclude fees, spread and slippage.

For CSV import, select the matching replay period. Include 145–5,000 historical XAUUSD M1 or M5 candles ending before today (UTC). Use time, open, high, low and close columns with Unix or time-zone-qualified ISO timestamps.

Learning model

How the model learns and counts pips

The model finds the 15 most similar earlier candles from the same feed and timeframe. It compares candle body, three-bar price change, closing position and range relative to the average five-bar range. If at least 10 of the 15 moved the same way by the next close, the model calls a paper buy or sell. Otherwise it waits. A match count describes these past examples only.

Projected pips use the earlier next-close movements scaled to the current replay’s recent range. The middle 50% spans the 25th to 75th percentile. Larger moves and losses can occur. A pip is the USD-per-ounce increment you select. All results exclude the spread, fees and slippage.

Each new candle reveals one outcome. The browser keeps up to 1,200 completed examples per feed and timeframe. Accuracy covers directional calls in the latest 100 evaluated observations, counting flat outcomes as misses. Warm-up history supplies examples. Only first-time, previously issued calls are graded. Importing a CSV starts a separate run for that file.

Indicator guide

How the minute check works

The monitor reads XAUUSD M1 candles from biquote over HTTPS, with Deriv as a second candle source. Analysis uses one provider at a time. The chart shows the source and checks for newly closed candles each minute. An M5 candle covers a UTC block such as 10:00–10:05 and appears only after all five consecutive minutes arrive. The spot card streams Deriv ticks while available or displays Gold API’s separately timestamped USD quote, checked every 30 seconds. The M1 close card shows the last completed minute. Spot and candle feeds can quote different prices.

Each indicator starts again after a gap in the series. A dash means the required readings are missing. After a feed interruption or an old close, the monitor labels the data and pauses alerts. TradingView shows ICE’s separate indicative quote, so the two charts can differ.
